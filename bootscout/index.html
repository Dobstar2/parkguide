The recognition is strongest when the camera can see distinctive details.

1

Photograph the front

2

Use the label when needed

For electronics, tools and appliances, photograph the model sticker for an exact search.

3

Check the result

AI can be wrong. Confirm the model, edition, size and included accessories against sold listings.

4

Match sold condition

Do not compare a damaged loose item with a boxed tested example.
